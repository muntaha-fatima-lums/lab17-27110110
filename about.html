Our story

One oven, one family recipe and a lot of early mornings.

What started as birthday cakes for friends grew into a small home bakery that now bakes for families across Lahore.

We still make everything by hand, in small batches, with real butter and fresh local ingredients. If it wasn't baked that morning, we don't sell it.
